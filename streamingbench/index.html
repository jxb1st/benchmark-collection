StreamingBench

Questions asked at a timestamp of a stream: real-time visual understanding (10 tasks), omni-source understanding (4), contextual understanding (2), sequential QA, and proactive output where the model must speak when an event happens.

Statistics

  • 4,500 questions on 900 videos, five per video. Each question is asked at a point in time, and the model may only use the video up to that point.
  • Five parts: real-time visual understanding (2,500 questions), omni-source understanding (1,000, with sound), contextual understanding (500), proactive output (250: the model must speak up when something happens) and sequential QA (250: several questions in a row on one video).
  • Videos are about ten minutes long (mean 609 s in the real-time part).
