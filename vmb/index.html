VideoMemBench

Multi-turn conversations about one video where later turns depend on earlier ones: episodic dependencies refer to what was seen, answer dependencies refer to what the model said.

Statistics

  • 326 videos, one conversation each: 2,476 question turns in all, usually 8 per video (4 to 21).
  • 1,387 turns can only be answered by remembering earlier turns; 1,089 can be answered from the video alone.

Two kinds of memory (one turn can have several links)

  • Episodic1,200 linksthe turn points back to something seen earlier in the video
  • Answer594 linksthe turn points back to what the model itself answered earlier

Turn types

  • open structured1,709 turnsfree answer with a reference and accepted paraphrases
  • multi choice question603 turnsfour options
  • true or false question164 turnsyes or no

Evaluation

Per turn
Each turn \(t\) is graded \(c(t)\in\{0,1\}\). Multiple choice and true/false by extracting the option; open answers by a judge model (GPT-5.4 mini) against the reference answers.
TurnAcc
Accuracy over all turns \(T\).
\[\mathrm{TurnAcc}=\frac{100}{|T|}\sum_{t\in T}c(t)\]
EpiDepAcc
Accuracy over the turns with an episodic dependency (the turn refers to something seen earlier); the parent turn need not be answered correctly.
\[\mathrm{EpiDepAcc}=\frac{100}{|T_{\mathrm{epi}}|}\sum_{t\in T_{\mathrm{epi}}}c(t)\]
AnsDepAcc
Accuracy over the turns with an answer dependency; a turn counts only if it and every earlier answer it builds on, \(\mathrm{anc}(t)\), are correct.
\[\mathrm{AnsDepAcc}=\frac{100}{|T_{\mathrm{ans}}|}\sum_{t\in T_{\mathrm{ans}}}c(t)\prod_{a\in\mathrm{anc}(t)}c(a)\]
Overall
Mean of the three.
\[\mathrm{Overall}=\frac{\mathrm{TurnAcc}+\mathrm{EpiDepAcc}+\mathrm{AnsDepAcc}}{3}\]
