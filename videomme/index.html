Video-MME

Multiple-choice QA over YouTube videos of three length groups (under 2 min, 4 to 15 min, 30 to 60 min), 6 domains and 30 subcategories, with 12 task types; subtitles and audio are optional inputs.

Statistics

  • 900 YouTube videos: 300 short (under 2 min), 300 medium (4 to 15 min), 300 long (30 to 60 min).
  • 2,700 multiple-choice questions, three per video, four options each. 744 videos also have subtitles.

Six domains

  • Knowledge810 questions
  • Life Record630 questions
  • Sports Competition450 questions
  • Film & Television360 questions
  • Artistic Performance360 questions
  • Multilingual90 questions

  • Object Reasoning454 questions
  • Object Recognition354 questions
  • Information Synopsis323 questions
  • Action Recognition313 questions
  • Action Reasoning285 questions
  • Counting Problem268 questions
  • Attribute Perception222 questions
  • Temporal Reasoning177 questions
  • OCR Problems139 questions
  • Spatial Reasoning56 questions
  • Temporal Perception55 questions
  • Spatial Perception54 questions

Evaluation

Accuracy
Four-option multiple choice; the letter is extracted from the reply with a regular expression, no judge model.
Two settings
Without subtitles, and with the subtitles that belong to the sampled frames.
Reported by
Short, medium and long videos, and overall.
