InteractionBench

A real-time interaction system watches a stream and decides at every moment whether to speak. Each item pairs a video with a question or a standing instruction and timed ground-truth responses: what should be said and the earliest moment it is warranted. Six tasks and 3,780 timed responses; 69 items require silence for the whole stream, and 53 near-miss suites pair counted events with look-alike moments that must not count. Scored on content accuracy, timing accuracy (5 s tolerance) and silence compliance.

Statistics

  • 812 videos, 15 s to 16 min long (median 144 s).
  • 1,060 items. An item is one request about one video, plus the correct answers and the time each answer is due.
  • 626 requests are worded as a question (“What does the white arrow refer to?”), 434 as an instruction (“Let me know when the traffic light for the lane turns green”).
  • Timing type A, 316 items: the question comes at some moment and the answer is due right away.
  • Type B, 338 items: the request comes early and one answer is due later, when the video shows it (“Which was opened first, Task Manager or Device Manager?”, answered at 21 s).
  • Type C, 406 items: the request comes early and an answer is due every time the event happens (“How many times does Red Bull appear?”, answered 14 times with a running count).
  • 69 items are traps: what they ask about never happens in the video, so the right behaviour is to stay silent. 688 items also have a multiple-choice version of the question.
  • 3,711 answers in all. Most items have one; counting items have up to 125.
  • Answers are free text with a time stamp, not multiple choice. For scoring, 688 items have a multiple-choice key (637 with six options, 48 with two) and the reply is matched to an option; 157 counting items are scored on the exact final count; 107 commentary items are scored segment by segment against the reference lines; 39 other free-text answers are graded by a judge model; 69 items are scored on staying silent.
  • Alert313 items272 with a multiple-choice key, 39 silence, 2 free text
  • Track192 items157 running counts, 30 silence, 4 free text, 1 with a multiple-choice key
  • Recall163 items154 with a multiple-choice key, 9 free text
  • Look159 items147 with a multiple-choice key, 12 free text
  • Time126 items114 with a multiple-choice key, 12 free text
  • Commentate107 items107 free-form commentary
