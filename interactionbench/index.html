InteractionBench

A real-time interaction system watches a stream and decides at every moment whether to speak. Each item pairs a video with a question or a standing instruction and timed ground-truth responses: what should be said and the earliest moment it is warranted. Six tasks and 3,780 timed responses; 69 items require silence for the whole stream, and 53 near-miss suites pair counted events with look-alike moments that must not count. Scored on content accuracy, timing accuracy (5 s tolerance) and silence compliance.

Statistics

  • 812 videos, 15 s to 15:40 long (median 2:24). 1,060 items, each one request about one video with its correct answers and the time each answer is due; 3,711 answers in all (most items have one, counting items up to 125).

  • Free-form commentary107 itemsone short line per step or scene; scored segment by segment
  • Free text39 itemsa short answer, graded by a judge model
  • Silence69 itemswhat is asked about never happens; the right behaviour is to say nothing

Evaluation

Content accuracy (Acc)
Is the matched reply right? Multiple-choice items by the chosen option, counting items by the exact final count, free-text items by a judge model, commentary segment by segment.
Timing accuracy (TA)
Each of the \(N\) reference events gets credit that falls linearly with the delay \(d_n\) of its matched reply and reaches zero at \(\Delta = 5\ \mathrm{s}\); a reply in the same second as the evidence is on time, a missed event earns nothing.
\[\mathrm{TA}=\frac{100}{N}\sum_{n=1}^{N}\Big(1-\frac{d_n}{\Delta}\Big)^{\!+},\qquad \Delta=5\ \mathrm{s}\]
Silence compliance (SC)
\(V\) is the set of unnecessary replies: premature ones (before the first event) and repeated ones (a second reply inside the same event window). On the 69 negative items every reply is a violation.
\[\mathrm{SC}=100\,\Big(1-\frac{|V|}{N}\Big)^{\!+}\]
Overall
Per item, the content score averaged with the harmonic mean of timing and silence; the dataset score is the mean over items.
\[\mathrm{Overall}=\frac{\mathrm{Acc}+H(\mathrm{TA},\mathrm{SC})}{2},\qquad H(a,b)=\frac{2ab}{a+b}\]
Near-miss suite pass rate
Over the 53 suites: \(h_j = 1\) if the counted events of suite \(j\) got timing credit, \(s_j = 1\) if nothing was said within 5 s of every near miss of the suite.
\[\mathrm{PairSel}=\frac{100}{53}\sum_{j=1}^{53} h_j\,s_j\]
Reported separately
False alarms on negative streams, and wall-clock latency per poll.
