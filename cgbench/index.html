CG-Bench

Clue-grounded QA over long videos: every question carries the clue intervals that answer it, so accuracy can be checked against where the model looked (clue accuracy, long accuracy, clue mIoU, open-ended).

Statistics

  • 1,219 videos from Bilibili and YouTube, 9:08 to 1:45:25 long (mean 27:41).
  • 12,129 multiple-choice questions with five to eight options. Every question also marks its clue: the part of the video that holds the answer, usually one clip (median 1, up to 45) of about 9 s.

Evaluation

Source: paper section 3.3.

long-acc.
Multiple-choice accuracy with the whole video as input (128 frames).
clue-acc.
Accuracy with only the annotated clue interval as input (32 frames).
CRR, clue recovery rate
min(long-acc., clue-acc.)/clue-acc.: how much of the clue-only accuracy survives when the clue is buried in the long video.
White-box: mIoU, rec.@IoU, acc.@IoU
The model also outputs the clue interval. mIoU: mean temporal overlap with the ground truth; rec.@IoU: recall of clue intervals at overlap thresholds 0.1 to 0.5, averaged; acc.@IoU: correct option and sufficient overlap, averaged over the same thresholds.
Open-ended accuracy
Free-text answers judged yes or no by GPT-4o against the reference.
